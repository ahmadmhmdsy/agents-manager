## Risks
| Risk | Severity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one in line numbers | low | Existing `no-trailing-h1` uses `lines.length` for 1-indexed line. New rule should use the actual line index of the second H1, not `i+1` blindly. |
| Col always 1 | low | All H1 lines start at col 1 by definition; no risk. |
| False positives on `## ...` lines | medium | The user said "H1 followed immediately by another H1" — strictly `# ` only, not `## `. Must match `/^# /` not `/^#+ /`. |
