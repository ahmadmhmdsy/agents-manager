# Brand Guidelines — Atlas

**Read this first**. One document a new team member reads on day one.

## 1. Logo

**Placeholder until ILLUSTRATE-mode dispatch delivers the real logo.** See `logo/logo-placeholder.svg`.

Rules when the real logo arrives:
- Clear space: minimum = height of the logo mark on all sides.
- Minimum size: 32px wide for digital, 12mm for print.
- Don't rotate, recolor (outside the approved palette), or apply effects.
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without a backing surface.

## 2. Color

See `color-palette.md`.

Quick reference:
- Primary green: `#2D4A2B` (leaves, freshness, growth)
- Primary deep: `#1A2F19` (depth, rooted, mature)
- Accent gold: `#C9851E` (warmth, harvest, sun) — use sparingly, ≤10% of any layout
- Warm cream bg: `#F8F4EC`
- Ink: `#1A1814`

## 3. Typography

See `typography.md`.

- Display: Playfair Display (heading only)
- Body: Inter (everything else)
- Mono: JetBrains Mono (lot numbers, technical specs)

## 4. Voice and tone

See `voice-and-tone.md`.

Three words: confident, specific, unhurried.

## 5. Photography

Style:
- Natural light. No studio flash.
- Origin shots: hands holding cherries, washing stations, drying beds, farm landscapes.
- Process shots: roaster drum, scales, cupping bowls.
- People: working. Not posed.
- Color palette: warm, slightly desaturated. Avoid over-filtered or oversaturated.

## 6. Illustration

If used:
- Hand-drawn feel, line + wash.
- Botanical subjects: coffee plants, cherries, leaves, mountains.
- No flat geometric shapes, no emoji-as-illustration.

## 7. Motion (when produced)

- Slow, considered. 320-480ms transitions.
- Easing: decelerate for enters, accelerate for exits. No bouncing.
- Use motion to reveal — never to decorate.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Don't (the 10-second list)

- No emoji as design elements.
- No exclamation marks in copy.
- No "craft" / "artisanal" / "premium" / "luxurious" without specificity.
- No more than 2 type families on a single page.
- No inline hex outside the palette tokens.
- No animations without `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.
- No stock photography that doesn't match the photography style.
- No "we" unless it's clear who "we" refers to.
- No apology for the product.
- No synthetic bold or italic.
