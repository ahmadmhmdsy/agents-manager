# Voice and Tone — Atlas

## Voice (constant)

Atlas is **confident, specific, unhurried**. We sound like a farmer explaining how they grew something — not a marketer explaining why you should buy it.

Reference character: the head roaster who's been at this for 20 years. They know what they're talking about. They don't oversell. They name things precisely.

## Tone (varies by context)

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Origin stories | Warm, specific, sensory. "Wet-milled at 6 AM. Fermented 48 hours. Dried on raised African beds." |
| Product descriptions | Factual, technical but readable. "Yirgacheffe, G1, 1900m, washed." |
| Marketing copy | Confident, no hype. State the fact; let the reader conclude. |
| Error messages | Direct, helpful, never blaming. |
| Empty states | Inviting, specific. "No coffees on the shelf yet. New harvest drops every other Thursday." |

## Vocabulary

### Use

- Origin (country, region, farm, lot)
- Process (washed, natural, honey, anaerobic)
- Roast (light, light-medium, with date)
- Varietal (Heirloom, Bourbon, Caturra, Geisha)
- Elevation (meters above sea level)
- Harvest year
- Tasting notes (concrete: stone fruit, cocoa, jasmine)

### Avoid

- "Craft" alone — what craft, specifically?
- "Artisanal" — overused, empty
- "Premium" — let the price and origin speak
- "Luxurious" — not who we are
- "Experience" — vague; what experience?
- "Curated" — by whom? with what intent?

## Sample phrases

| Bad | Better |
|---|---|
| "Our premium artisanal coffee experience" | "Single-origin Ethiopia, G1, 1900m, washed." |
| "Hand-crafted with love" | "Roasted in 12kg batches on a Probat 25." |
| "Discover the magic" | "Lot 47 is on the shelf. Tastes like bergamot and peach." |
| "We're sorry for any inconvenience" | "Subscription paused. Resume anytime from your account." |

## Don't

- Don't anthropomorphize: "Atlas believes..." — Atlas doesn't believe anything; the team does.
- Don't use exclamation marks.
- Don't apologize for the product.
