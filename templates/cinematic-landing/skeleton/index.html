٠١ — The Atelier

poured
by hand,
one at a time.

Each Maison Lumen candle is measured, melted, and set in small batches of thirty. We work in a single atelier — three editions, three scent worlds, no reissues.

  • Edition size30 candles per pour
  • Waxcoconut + apricot kernel, no paraffin
  • Wickcotton, lead-free, hand-centered
  • Burn≈ 48 hours, clean & even
  • Vesselhand-thrown stoneware, refillable
٠٢ — The Ritual

light one, read a little,
blow it out.

Our Sundays are written for slow rooms — a single wick, a paragraph or two, the room drawn around the smell of amber and fig leaf. You don't need to do anything with a Maison Lumen candle except let it burn for itself.
